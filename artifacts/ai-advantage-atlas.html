Where this comes from. The club's own directory listing: a name, an optional one-line bio and an optional location per member. Those three free-text fields were split into structured columns — city, region, country, continent, domain, role in the club, and a completeness score.

What is inferred. Country, domain and completeness are derived from the member's own words, not from searching the web for anyone. Treat them as a way to sort the room, not as verified fact. A ± marks a city name that exists in several countries, where the country shown is a best guess.

Blank means unknown. 65% of the club wrote no bio and 49% gave no location. A blank domain says the bio had no professional signal in it — never that the person does nothing.
